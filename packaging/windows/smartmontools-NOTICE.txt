smartmontools bundled component
================================

This package includes the standalone smartctl executable and drive database from
smartmontools 7.5. smartmontools is licensed under the GNU General Public License,
version 2 or (at your option) any later version.

Upstream project: https://github.com/smartmontools/smartmontools
Release: https://github.com/smartmontools/smartmontools/releases/tag/RELEASE_7_5

The installed smartmontools/COPYING.txt contains the license. The complete
corresponding upstream source archive is installed as
smartmontools/smartmontools-7.5-source.tar.gz.

smartctl is executed as a separate process. It is not linked into host-monitor.
